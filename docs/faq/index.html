FAQ

Straight answers.

The questions every institution asks us first. Yours not here? Ask us directly.

How much does it cost?
Ready products are a monthly or annual subscription sized to your institution; custom software is priced per project after we understand the work. Either way, you get a clear quote before anything starts - and our pricing is built for small institutions, not big companies.
How long does it take to get started?
A working first version in weeks, not months. Ready products deploy fastest; custom software takes a little longer depending on the workflow.
Do you train our staff?
Yes. We train on-site or on call, in the language your team speaks, and we stay available after go-live.
Does it work on phones?
Yes. Every product works on phones as well as computers, because most of your staff will use it on one.
Who owns our data?
You do. You can export all of it at any time, and if you ever leave, your data leaves with you.
Can a ready product be customized?
Yes. If a ready product is 80% right, we shape the remaining 20% to your process instead of making you change how you work.
What does the AI actually do?
It writes daily summaries, sends WhatsApp reminders, predicts demand and stock, and answers plain-language questions about your own data. See the AI page for the full list.

Tell us what is slowing you down.

We will show you a demo on your own data - no slideware, no obligation.
