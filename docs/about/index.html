About

A small team building software people actually use.

Maharashtra, India. We build for the schools, shops and small hotels that still run on paper registers, scattered spreadsheets and WhatsApp.

Why we exist

The software gap nobody bothered to close.

The software that could help small institutions is either built for big companies, priced for big companies, or abandoned after the sale. Sahaaj Systems exists to close that gap: ready products priced for small institutions, custom software shaped to real processes, and support that stays after go-live.

How we work

  • We sit with you and map how work actually happens today.
  • You get a working first version in weeks, not months.
  • We train your staff on-site or on call, in the language your team speaks.
  • We stay: local support, fixes and new features as you grow.
